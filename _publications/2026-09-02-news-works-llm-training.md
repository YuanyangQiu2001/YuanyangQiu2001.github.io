Chinese title: 《论大模型训练中新闻作品使用行为的法律性质判断》

The article proposes a conduct-first approach to the copyright analysis of news works in LLM training: it applies the idea–expression dichotomy to the *use* rather than to the *work*, distinguishing expressive from non-expressive uses during training, and pairs that behavioral dimension with a distinction between factual and original news to produce a four-quadrant typology. It stresses that characterizing a use is not the same as assigning liability.
